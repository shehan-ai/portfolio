​Abstract
​This academic assessment portfolio compiles the coursework, collaborative projects, and reflective evaluations completed for the module SC1153: Introduction to Biotechnology at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT). The portfolio structured across four core assignments demonstrates academic progression in theoretical biotechnology, media communication, industry research, and bioethics:

​Assignment 1: A foundational group presentation exploring core biotechnology applications.

​Assignment 2: An educational video project focused on Mycelium Leather, detailing sustainable bioproducts through scripting, field filming, and an expert interview with Prof. Colin.

​Assignment 3: A career trajectory study based on SLIIT library research profiling Professor Sangeeta Bhatia as a biotech role model and mapping a three-stage personal career development roadmap (Score: 75/100).

​Assignment 4: A formal academic debate on the ethics of mandatory forensic DNA databases held in Hall G1106 under Mr. Shanitha M, serving as Opposition Leader and securing an audience majority vote victory.

​Throughout this module, I developed key technical competencies in biological sciences alongside soft skills, including public speaking, media production, rapid speech preparation under tight deadlines, strategic argumentation, and collaborative leadership.

​Objectives
​Overall Portfolio Objective
​To systematically document academic growth, practical skills, and reflective insights gained across all theoretical and applied assessments in SC1153: Introduction to Biotechnology.

​Assignment One: Group Presentation
​To research and present foundational biotechnology concepts collaboratively.

​To build effective visual slides and practice peer presentation dynamics in a group setting.

​Assignment Two: Educational Video Project (Mycelium Leather)
​To translate complex scientific principles of sustainable bioproducts into an engaging educational video format.

​To gain practical skills in scientific communication, scriptwriting, field filming, and conducting expert interviews.

​Assignment Three: Career Trajectory & Role Model Profile
​To investigate the career path and scientific contributions of a prominent biotechnologist (Prof. Sangeeta Bhatia) using SLIIT library research tools.

​To analyze industry trends and construct a personal short-, medium-, and long-term career roadmap in biotechnology.

​Assignment Four: Bioethics Debate
​To critically evaluate ethical, legal, and social implications surrounding mandatory forensic DNA databases.

​To strengthen formal academic debate skills, persuasive public speaking, and spontaneous counter-argumentation.
